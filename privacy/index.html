Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

8¾ Ball works offline. You can use it without an account. The app does not collect or send personal data to us or to third parties. It contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.

What the app uses

The app processes motion readings on your iPhone to detect shakes, flips, and tilts. It does not record or send those readings. You think of your question yourself. The app does not use the microphone or record your question.

What stays on your iPhone

The app saves your language, season, answer mode, and sound and vibration settings. It also saves the names and answers you write in My Ball. The app keeps this information in its local storage and does not sync it to a server.

How to remove saved information

Delete a custom set in Settings under Edit My Ball. To remove all locally stored app data, choose Delete App on your iPhone. If you use device backups, Apple and your iPhone settings control those backups.

Support and privacy questions

You can reach us at gitznasoftdev@gmail.com. If you email us, we use your address and the details you send to respond and troubleshoot. You can ask us to delete that correspondence by writing to the same address.
