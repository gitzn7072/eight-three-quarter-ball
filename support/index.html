Good questions. Clear answers.

How do I get an answer?+

Think of a yes-or-no question, then tap Get an answer or shake your iPhone. You can also hold it face down and flip it face up. An answer appears through the smoke.

Shaking does nothing. What should I do?+

Close the app’s Settings, then try one clear back-and-forth shake. You can always tap Get an answer instead.

Why is there no sound?+

The app respects Silent mode. Turn off Silent mode on your iPhone, check the volume, and turn on sound in the app’s Settings. You can also enjoy the ball with sound off.

How do I write my own answers?+

Open Settings and choose Edit My Ball. Create a set with at least two different answers, then choose My Ball under Answer mode. To delete a set, touch and hold it in the list of sets and choose Delete.

Can I change the language or answer style?+

Yes. Open the app’s Settings to choose your language and Answer mode. Classic and Fantasy offer two different styles of answers. The website language buttons change this website only.

The season looks wrong. Can I change it?+

In Settings, choose Auto under Season to follow the date and your hemisphere. You can also choose a season manually.

Which devices can run the app?+

8¾ Ball is designed for iPhone and requires iOS 18 or later.
